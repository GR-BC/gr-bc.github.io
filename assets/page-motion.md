# First-visit motion

`page-motion.js` supplies one shared entrance system for the homepage's logical HTML parts. It is deferred from the document head so static content can be registered before asynchronously mounted modules arrive. The selectors cover the hero, every section heading, figures, the trajectory shelf and details, weighting cases, experiment cards, comparison table, ablation details, robustness rack, citation, and footer.

Each part enters once per page visit with a 650 ms fade and small individual CSS `translate`, with at most 260 ms of sibling staggering. Figures with their own spatial animation use an opacity-only entrance. Individual translate leaves existing hover transforms intact. Capturing pointer/focus handlers finish an entrance before an interaction measures its geometry. The native trajectory shelf's completed card morph counts as the card's entrance, avoiding a second fade after the shared-element transition.

Title-side explanatory paragraphs, the hero tagline, state-diagram captions, and newly exposed pipeline/dip explanations receive a typewriter reveal. DOM Ranges measure original grapheme positions; a moving polygon clip reveals the original paragraph without splitting letters, changing line breaks, inserting replacement copy, or moving content below it. A small caret follows the current glyph. The complete original copy remains accessible and selectable. Typing takes 850–2350 ms plus a short initial delay, with a small punctuation pause. It pauses offscreen or in a hidden document, and remeasures after resize without restarting. Previously read copy does not retype on scroll or when an equivalent explanation is recreated.

Intersection observers trigger only when content enters the viewport. A filtered mutation observer discovers asynchronously mounted HTML and newly opened panels, while ignoring animated SVG geometry, readout text updates, and transition ghosts. Hidden/inert or inactive explanation panels are not prepared until exposed. Existing module-owned curve drawing, node magnification, hover, playback, and replay behavior are retained.

Without enhancement, the page and former `.reveal` blocks remain visible. Reduced motion and printing immediately expose all copy and parts. Browser back/forward cache preserves completed states. Refresh the first 12 SHA-256 characters in the CSS/JS URLs after edits; changing the base stylesheet also requires its matching version in `index.html`.
